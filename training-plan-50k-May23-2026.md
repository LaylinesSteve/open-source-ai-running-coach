**Verdict:** The plan is **doable**, but you’re a bit behind where 2024-you was when the build started. The main gaps are **run frequency** (you need to get to 3–4 runs/week) and **long-run ceiling** (you haven’t run 6+ mi yet this year). That makes Week 1 (16 mi, 6 mi long run) a real step up.

**What makes it doable anyway:** (1) You’ve already finished a 50k. (2) CrossFit is giving you a real aerobic and strength base. (3) You have 11 weeks. (4) You’re not coming from zero.

**What to watch:** Jumping straight to 6 mi long run and 16 mi in Week 1 from 2.7 mi last run is where injury or burnout risk is highest. If the first week feels like too much, use the **“Softer start”** below for Weeks 1–2, then rejoin the plan at Week 3. You’ll still have 9 weeks to build to the same peak; your 2024 build had some down weeks too.

## Tips (from your 2024 pattern)

1. **Trail time:** Get on trail for every long run and at least one other run per week when you can (Lapham, race course, etc.).
2. **Walk when you need to:** You used “some running, some walking” in 2024; same approach in long runs and on race day.
3. **CrossFit:** Keep 2–3x/week; avoid heavy legs the day before your Saturday long run (e.g. skip or go light Friday).
4. **Back-to-backs (optional):** In 2024 you did some two-day blocks (e.g. 16 + 3). If you want one late in the plan, Week 7 or 8: long run Saturday, 3–4 mi easy Sunday.
5. **Race week:** Short, easy runs only Tue/Thu; rest or walk Friday; race Saturday.
6. **Fueling:** Practice race-day nutrition and hydration on 14–18 mi long runs.

---

## If You Need to Adjust

- **Behind on volume:** Add 1–2 mi to easy runs; don’t jump the long run more than 1–2 mi from the prior week.
- **Ahead of schedule:** You can add 1–2 mi to a long run or one extra short run; keep the taper as written.
- **Niggles:** Swap a run for walking or pool; keep the long run easy or shorten by 1–2 mi and extend the next week.
- **Event conflict:** Shift the long run to Sunday when needed; keep the same weekly totals and long-run progression.

You’ve already shown you can build from low volume to a 50k in a similar timeframe; this plan matches that path to May 23.
